USV Acheron // Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026.

Hullbreach collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. Nothing you do in the game is sent to the developer.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, such as stick opacity and volume.
  • Your save: progress, upgrades, crew tags and any run in progress.

What Apple handles

The game uses three Apple services. Each works under your own Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy.

  • iCloud. Your progress is stored in your own iCloud so it follows you between your devices. The developer cannot read it.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports your achievements and your fastest winning run to it. Your time appears on the public leaderboard under your Game Center name. The developer can see that leaderboard, as any player can, and can remove a score. If you are not signed in, nothing is reported.
  • Purchases. The Full Ship purchase is made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. The developer receives sales totals from Apple and never sees your name or payment details.

If you email support

Your message and your email address are used only to answer you. They are not shared and not added to any list. Ask, and they will be deleted.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

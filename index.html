HULLBREACH

Hullbreach is a top-down action roguelike for iPhone and iPad. This page answers the common questions. For anything else, write to hullbreachgame@icloud.com.

How do I play?

Your left thumb moves. The three buttons under your right thumb strike, dash and fire your Special. In the Cargo Bay, walk up the marked lane into the airlock to start a run. After each fight, walk through an open door to enter the next compartment. A game controller works too.

Stick opacity, dead zone, screen shake and high-contrast attack warnings are under Settings on the title screen and in the pause menu.

What is free and what is paid?

Deck 4 is free, boss included. One purchase, the Full Ship, unlocks Decks 2 and 0. There are no ads, no timers and nothing else to buy.

I bought the Full Ship and it is locked

Open the Cargo Bay and tap FULL SHIP, then RESTORE PURCHASE. Use the Apple Account that bought it, or one in the same Family Sharing group. The lower decks open from the next run you start. If a purchase is waiting for approval, for example through Ask to Buy, the ship unlocks by itself once it is approved.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does my progress follow me to another device?

Yes, when iCloud is on for your Apple Account. Black-Box Data, suit upgrades, weapons and crew tags sync between your devices. A run in progress stays on the device you started it on.

The game is too hard

It is meant to be, at first. The Black-Box Data you salvage on a run buys permanent suit upgrades, and your first lost clone recovers a crew tag that revives you once per run. Spend the data at the Engram Printer in the Cargo Bay before you go back in.

Something broke

Write to hullbreachgame@icloud.com with your device model, your iOS version and what happened just before. A screenshot helps.
